05 / WAREHOUSE DESIGN

Three grains. Shared dimensions.

Separate fact tables preserve each source's meaning and prevent participants from multiplying when measures, scans and events are combined.

  1. 01 / SOURCEBIDS research files

    TSV tables, JSON dictionaries and local scan paths.

  2. 02 / RAWTraceable landing

    Original values, source rows and small-file SHA-256 hashes.

  3. 03 / COREConformed dimensions

    Participant, instrument, measure, scan task, scanner and dataset.

  4. 04 / MARTSPublic summaries

    SQL aggregates exported as a versioned website snapshot.
